What it is

A managed service in which Start9 operates self-hosted infrastructure on behalf of an organization. The organization owns the hardware and the data; Start9 builds the servers, runs them, keeps them updated, and provides support. The infrastructure runs on the organization's own equipment; the difference from the rest of the catalog is that Start9 handles operation and maintenance.

Who it's for

Organizations that need private, self-hosted infrastructure without running IT in-house:

  • Newsrooms
  • Clinics
  • Law firms
  • Family offices
  • Nonprofits
  • Privacy-sensitive businesses

What's included

  • Build-to-order hardware. Servers configured for the organization's requirements, assembled and warrantied.
  • Onboarding and staff training. Start9 sets up the systems and trains staff to use them. No prior self-hosting experience is required.
  • 24/7/365 support. On-demand access to the Start9 support team, every day of the year.
  • Maintenance, updates, and monitoring. Start9 keeps the systems patched and monitored. Updates remain explicit and reviewed; nothing is applied without the organization's involvement.

How it works

  1. Scope. The organization describes its requirements and what it needs to run privately.
  2. Build. Start9 designs the setup and builds the hardware to order.
  3. Onboard and train. Start9 installs the systems, hands them over, and trains staff to use them.
  4. Support. Start9 maintains and supports the systems for the term of the agreement.
